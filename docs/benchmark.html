Letter Scramble

Below, we've used a dataset of the letters 'C,a,r,t,o,n,s'. The accepting states are all of the words in the Cambridge English dictionary that you can make as an anagram of these letters, starting with the letter C. The Markov chain shows us the likeliness of transitioning between letters based on these words in the dictionary.
